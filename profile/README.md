### Hello there 👋

We are the Home of Laboratory Automation Research (HóLAR) at the University of Utah, a team in Professor Taylor Sparks' research group. We develop autonomous laboratories, self-driving laboratories (SDLs) and materials acceleration platforms.

Our current work asks how a self-driving materials laboratory should reuse knowledge from sources whose fidelity is known only in relative terms: density functional theory at different levels, machine-learned interatomic potentials, molecular dynamics, experimental compilations and materials databases. The articles are collected at [haikusdl.kinstonackolf.info/articles](https://haikusdl.kinstonackolf.info/articles/), in four chapters and a capstone. Each repository below holds the code and data behind one article, and only code and data: the paper sources are not kept here. Every figure script runs from a fresh copy of its repository. Further papers in each chapter are in preparation and will be listed once they are complete.
