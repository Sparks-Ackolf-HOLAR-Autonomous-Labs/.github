### Hello there 👋

We are the Home of Laboratory Automation Research (HóLAR) at the University of Utah, a team in Professor Taylor Sparks' research group. We build autonomous laboratories, self-driving laboratories (SDLs) and materials acceleration platforms.

Our current work asks how a self-driving materials laboratory should reuse knowledge from sources whose fidelity is known only in relative terms: density functional theory at different levels, machine-learned interatomic potentials, molecular dynamics, experimental compilations and materials databases. A campaign has to borrow that knowledge, revise it when the evidence changes, scale it to more candidates than it can test one by one, and share it with other groups, instruments and people. The articles follow these four problems in four chapters and are collected at [haikusdl.kinstonackolf.info/articles](https://haikusdl.kinstonackolf.info/articles/).

Each repository below holds the code and data behind one article, and nothing else; the paper sources are kept elsewhere. Every figure script runs from a fresh copy of its repository. Rows marked "in preparation" are still being completed and will be marked ready once their article and repository are final.
